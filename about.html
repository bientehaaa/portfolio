About

A bridge between the question and the solution.

I am Alireza Kazemzadeh, a Business Analyst and Business Systems Analyst focused on the space where business needs, processes, data, and technology meet.

My perspective

Make complexity easier to work with.

Business and technology work best when people have a shared understanding of the problem, the process around it, and the practical constraints that shape a solution.

My approach is to ask clear questions, bring the right details into view, and create useful artifacts that support conversation and delivery. That may mean clarifying a workflow, surfacing a data question, documenting a requirement, or helping a team prepare to validate a change.

The goal is not documentation for its own sake. It is a clearer basis for thoughtful decisions and practical action.

Positioning

Business analysis, systems thinking, and technology context.

A portfolio built for hiring managers, technology professionals, and potential consulting conversations.

Working principles

How I approach the work.

01

Start with context

Understand the people, purpose, processes, and constraints before moving toward a solution.

02

Make the implicit visible

03

Keep delivery practical

Focus on work that is useful to the people making, validating, and using a change.

04

Connect the parts

Consider business needs, processes, systems, and information together—not in isolation.
